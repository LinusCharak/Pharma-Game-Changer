What is fatigue?

Fatigue is the feeling of extreme tiredness, exhaustion or lethargy resulting from mental or physical stress or illness. Fatigue that does not go away with age is called chronic fatigue.  In other words, it’s a disease characterised by profound fatigue, sleep abnormalities, pain and other symptoms that are made worse by exertion.

 

Causes of Fatigue-

Scientists don't know exactly what causes chronic fatigue syndrome. It may be a combination of factors that affect people who were born with a predisposition for the disorder.  However Aging & Oxidative Stress is the most common cause of CF which leads to modification in mitochondrial proteins, lipids and DNA (mitochondrial dysfunction) and causing chronic fatigue. Mitochondrial dysfunction or degeneration due to various causes results in decrease of AMPK production. Shortage of AMPK (Adenosine Monophosphate Kinase) leads to reduction in ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) levels. ATP is a cell’s primary source of energy. Lack of ATP results in fatigue.
