What is Indication of FEMIFORTE?

  • Excessive vaginal discharge due to fungal / bacterial / protozoal infection
  • Abnormal / excessive vaginal discharge associated with pregnancy
  • Recurrent vaginitis

 

What is Vaginitis?

Vaginitis, also known as vulvovaginitis, is inflammation of the vagina and vulva. Symptoms may include itching, burning, pain, discharge, and a bad smell.

 

What are symptoms of Vaginitis?

Symptoms of vaginitis are vaginal itching, swelling or inflammation.

 

What are cause of Vaginitis?

Depends on the type of vaginitis such as:

  • Bacterial vaginosis - it occurs due to change in the balance of vaginal bacteria.
  • Yeast infections - due to overgrowth of fungal organisms.
  • Trichomoniasis- a sexually transmitted infection.
  • Vaginal atrophy - Inflammation of the vagina due to thinning and shrinking of the tissues.
  • Other factors such as douching, vaginal spray, perfume, detergents may cause non-infectious vaginitis.
  • Risk factors include:
  • Hormonal changes due pregnancy, menopause or on birth control pills may increase the risk.
  • Sexual activity
  • Multiple sexual partners
  • Uncontrolled diabetes
  • Certain medications such as antibiotics or steroids
  • Ill-fitting or damp under garments
