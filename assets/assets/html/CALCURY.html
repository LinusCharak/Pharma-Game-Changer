What are the Indications and its meaning?

Urinary calculi, Burning micturition & pain    

Urinary calculi - means renal or kidney stone.

The basic constituents of most Kidney stones are Calcium salts, Uric acid, Cystine, & Struvite (MgNH4PO4). Calcium oxalate & Calcium phosphate are common molecules of Calcium stones. They make up 75 - 85% of the total & may be admixed in the same stone.

  • Calcium stones are more common in men. The average age of onset is the third decade. Approximately 60% of people who form a single Calcium stone eventually form another within the next 10 years. The average rate of new stone formation in patients who have had a previous stone is about one stone every 2 or 3 years. Calcium stone disease usually occurs in families.
  • Uric acid stones (cannot be seen in X-rays) are also more common in men.
  • Cystine stones are uncommon, lemon yellow & sparkle. They can be seen in X-ray.
  • Struvite stones are common in women & in patients who require chronic bladder catheterization. They result from UTIs with urease-producing bacteria, usually Proteus species. They are radiopaque & have a variable internal density.

 

What does CALCURY do?

Calcury offers lithotriptic action and prevents recurrence of renal stone

 

How does it work? Mechanism of Action

  1. Lithotriptic action is offered by-

Yavakshar, Gokshur & Pashanbhed- Disintegrates / reduces the size of calculi

Varun- Decreases calcium oxalate levels & Nephroprotective

Gokshur & Punarnava- Exhibits anti-inflammatory & analgesic action

Ikshumool & Gokshur- Diuretic action

  1. Prevents recurrence by-

Yavakshar & Gokshur- Dissolves residual calculi

Varun & Gokshur- Maintains crystalloid-colloid balance
