M2-TONE FORTE

(Non-invasive, Non-hormonal Approach to Excessive Uterine Bleeding)

 

What is M2-Tone Forte for?

M2-Tone Forte is fortified formulation to treat Menorrhagia, Metrorrhagia, Polymenorrhoea & IUCD induced excessive bleeding.

 

What is Menorrhagia?

Menorrhagia, also known as heavy menstrual bleeding, is a condition where a lady experiences excessive or prolonged menstrual bleeding

 

What is Metrorrhagia?

Menorrhagia, also known as heavy menstrual bleeding, is a condition where a lady experiences excessive or prolonged menstrual bleeding

 

What is Polymenorrhea?

Polymenorrhea is a menstrual disorder that causes frequent, short menstrual cycles, usually less than 21 days.

 

What is IUCD induced excessive bleeding?

Heavy bleeding is a common side effect of intrauterine contraceptive devices (IUCD). Also known as copper-T.
