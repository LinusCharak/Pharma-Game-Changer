What is cough?

Cough is a defensive & physiological reflex to protect the lower respiratory tract.

 

What is mechanism of cough?

A mechanism of cough is divided into four distinct phases: inspiratory, compressive, expiratory, and relaxation. These phases are characterized by deep inspiration, closure of the glottis, contraction of expiratory muscles with glottis opening, and relaxation of intercostal and abdominal muscles.

 

What are types of cough?

Cough is basically divided into two types: productive cough and non-productive cough.

 

What is productive cough?

Productive cough if the current cough accompanied by sputum discharge, also called cough with phlegm. Coughing up phlegm is a cough accompanied by mucus. Sputum that can come out of the nose and sinuses can also from the throat and lungs.

 

What is non-productive cough?

Non-productive cough it’s dry and does not contains phlegm so often also called a dry cough. Dry cough occurs because of the sensitivity due to the vibrating feathers inside the throat, for example if there is an allergy. Dry cough usually appears towards the end of flu symptoms or irritation due to dust and cigarette smoke.
