What are Superiority over Competitors?

Superiority over Synthetic SERMs (Raloxifene)

  • Natural Selective Estrogen Receptor Modulators (SERMs) are the drugs which act as a partial estrogen agonist and antagonist. It acts as an agonist on bone and endothelium while as an antagonist on reproductive system. They are used in the prevention of osteoporosis and to reduce the risk of breast cancer and atherosclerosis in postmenopausal women.
  • Synthetic SERMS like Raloxifene may cause certain side effects like blood clots in the legs, lungs, or eyes, leg swelling/pain, trouble breathing, chest pain, vision changes, birth defects etc.
  • Soya present in Evonova is a Natural SERM which does not cause any side-effects.

 

Advantages over ERT/HRT

  • Hormone replacement therapy (HRT) and estrogen replacement therapy (ERT) are used in the treatment of the symptoms of menopause and to prevent and treat postmenopausal osteoporosis.
  • ERT/HRT can cause certain side effects like breast tenderness, headaches, nausea, indigestion, abdominal pain, vaginal bleeding etc.
  • If taken for longer time, ERT might increase the risk of ovarian cancer.
  • ERT and HRT taken for extended periods can increase women’s risk of breast cancer and cardiovascular disease.
  • Evanova is uniquely balanced combination of Isoflavones (from soya), balancing herbs and antioxidants to maintain menopausal health.
  • Evanova effectively controls menopause to post-menopausal complication, without any side-effects.
