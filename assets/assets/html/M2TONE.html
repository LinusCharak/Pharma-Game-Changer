What is AUB?

Abnormal uterine bleeding is irregular, abnormal uterine bleeding that is not caused by a tumor, infection, or pregnancy

Characteristics of normal and abnormal menstruation:

 

Normal

Abnormal

Duration of flow

2 to 7 days average 5 days

Less than 2 or more than 7 days

Amount of flow

50 to 80 ml

More than 80 ml

Cycle days

21 to 35 days

Less than 21 or more than 35 days

 

Any deviation in normal frequency, duration or amount of menstruation and any bleeding at other times in the menstrual cycle or a cyclical perimenopausal bleeding or postmenopausal bleeding should be regarded as abnormal

 

What are different types of AUB?

Types of AUB

Bleeding Pattern

Menorrhagia or Hypermenorrhea

prolonged (> 7 days) / or excessive (>80 ml) uterine bleeding occurring at regular intervals.

Metrorrhagia

irregular or a cyclic bleeding (intermittent or continuous

Menometrorrhagia

irregular or a cyclic bleeding and of excessive amount

Menostaxis

regular periods but prolonged duration

Polymenorrhea or (Epimenorrhea)

frequent menstruation (<21 days), at regular intervals

Polymenorrhagia

frequent heavy periods

Intermenstual bleeding (commonly called "spotting)

uterine bleeding of variable amounts occurring between regular menstrual periods.

Breakthrough bleeding

spotting or mild bleeding during the intermenstrual period or during hormonal therapy

Hypomenorrhea

scanty menstruation

Oligomenorrhea

infrequent menstruation (>35 days)

How does M2Tone regulate menstruation?

M2-Tone acts on H-P-O axis & restores the natural balance between Hormones, Nutrition & Emotions.

  • M2-Tone contains ASHOKA which has the estrogen like action & acts by competitive inhibition of estrogen receptors
  • Because of this action it normalizes hormone levels, corrects the disturbed H-P-O axis & ultimately corrects the menstrual irregularity
  • During scanty bleeding when estrogen levels are low, M2-Tone will increase the estrogen levels while in excessive menstrual bleeding, when estrogen levels are high, it will decrease the levels
  • Thus, M2-Tone is effective in both scanty & excessive menstrual bleeding.
