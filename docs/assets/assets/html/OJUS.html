What are the Indications of OJUS?

Indigestion also known as Functional Dyspepsia

 

What is indigestion?

Indigestion is often a sign of an underlying problem, such as lack of digestive enzymes, gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), ulcers, or gallbladder disease, rather than a condition of its own. Also called dyspepsia, it is defined as a persistent or recurrent pain or discomfort in the upper abdomen.
