What is Free Radicals?

A free radical is an unstable molecule that can damage cells in the body. Free radicals are created when an atom or molecule gains or loses an electron. Also known as an atom with missing electron.

 

What is Oxidative Stress?

Oxidative stress is a condition that occurs when there is an imbalance of free radicals and antioxidants in the body. This imbalance can cause cell and tissue damage.

 

What are causes of Oxidative Stress?

Lifestyle factors: Poor diet, obesity, smoking, alcohol consumption, and certain medications

Environmental factors: Exposure to radiation, toxins, air pollution, pesticides, and sunlight

Physiological factors: Aging and exercise

 

Which diseases are caused due to Oxidative Stress?

Oxidative stress can contribute to many diseases, including:

Cancer: Oxidative stress is a factor in the development of cancer.

Cardiovascular disease: Oxidative stress is linked to cardiovascular disease, including atherosclerosis and coronary artery disease.

Neurological disorders: Oxidative stress is a factor in the development of neurological disorders, including Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, and amyotrophic lateral sclerosis.

Diabetes: Oxidative stress is a factor in the development of diabetes and diabetic complications, such as diabetic nephropathy and neuropathy.

Rheumatoid arthritis: Oxidative stress is linked to the development of rheumatoid arthritis, an autoimmune disease that causes chronic inflammation of the joints.

Cataracts: Oxidative stress can damage lens protein, leading to cataracts.

Male infertility: Oxidative stress is a major cause of DNA fragmentation in sperm, which can lead to male infertility.
