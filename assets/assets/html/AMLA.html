ANTI-OXIDANT SUPPORT

The antioxidant effect of amla means it helps protect your body from damage caused by harmful molecules called free radicals.

Free radicals are unstable and harmful molecules that are naturally produced in your body during normal processes like digestion, exercise, or when you're exposed to pollution, smoking, or stress. They speed up aging, weaken immunity, and can lead to diseases like diabetes, heart problems, and even cancer.

Amla is rich in vitamin C and polyphenols, which act like natural bodyguards. They neutralize these free radicals, reduce inflammation, and help your body stay healthy, youthful, and strong.
