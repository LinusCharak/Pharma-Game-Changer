Advantage of Livomyn over Liv.52

  • LIVOMYN contains therapeutic concentration of Bhumyamlaki (25mg) which is a hepatoprotective with antiviral properties and shows a powerful inhibitory effect on hepatitis B virus. LIV-52 does not contain Bhumyamlaki.
  • LIVOMYN is the only formulation with 70 mg Kalmegh standardized to 5% of andrographolides.Kalmegh is a potent cholagogue with powerful antiviral &antiinflammatory properties. Liv52 does not contain Kalmegh.
  • LIVOMYN Syrup and Drops have excellent taste as compared to Liv52..
  • Aloe barbadensis(kumari) in LIVOMYN is known to maintain the integrity of the hepatocytes, which constitute about 80% of the liver cells. LIVOMYN restores the integrity of hepatocytes and has the unique property to reverse the hepatotoxin–induced pre-fibrogenic events.Aloe is absent in LIV52..
  • LIVOMYN contains more potent antioxidants..
  • A nuclear medicine based non-invasive

        hepatobiliaryScintigraphy study proved that LIVOMYN improves HEE (Hepatic Extraction Efficiency) in drug-induced hepatotoxicity.  No such evidence is available for LIV52.

 

Advantage of Livomyn over Urodeoxycholic Acid Brands like Udiliv (Abbott)

  • Udiliv contains Ursodeoxsycholic acid which increases the production and flow of bile. LIVOMYN not only increases bile flow but also offers antiviral, anti-inflammatory, antioxidant action.
  • Ursodeoxycholic acid is available only in tablet form whereas LIVOMYN is available in a differential range of dosage forms (Tablets, Syrup, Drops) as per the need of the patients.
  • Cost of therapy per day is Low with LIVOMYN compared to Udiliv.

 

Advantage of Livomyn over Silymarin

  • Silymarin (SILYBON – MICRO) is is an active principle from the fruit of Silybummarianum, which contains a mixture of flavonolignans. It reduces the turnover of membrane phospholipids and stabilises the cell membranes of hepatocytes. It has potent antioxidant action and prevents lipid peroxidation.
  • The dosage of Silymarin is 35 mg / kg body weight, but the formulations available contain only 140 mg as tablets / 150 mg in 10 ml syrup. For therapeutic effect, 1750 mg of Silymarin will be required for a 50 kg adult. Thus, concentrations provided by SILYBON is sub – therapreutic.
  • A nuclear medicine based non-invasive HepatobiliaryScintigraphy study proved that LIVOMYN improves HEE (Hepatic Extraction Efficiency) in drug-induced hepatotoxicity. No such evidence is available for SILYBON.
