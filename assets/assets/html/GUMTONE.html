What is GUM TONE?

GUM TONE is a herbal astringent, which is useful in gingival (gum) disorders.

 

What are indications of GUM TONE?

1) Gingivitis

2) Prevention of plaque formation & dental caries

3) After scaling, to reduce dental pockets

4) Halitosis (bad breath)

What are Competitive advantages?

COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
Over G-32 – ALARSIN

GUM TONE Powder is a micro-pulverized, free-flowing, non-abrasive powder whereas G32 is in a tablet form and needs to be crushed before use.

GUM TONE Powder contains Babul & Phitkari which offers astringent & hemostatic action which helps in tightening of gums & to arrest bleeding immediately.

GUM TONE contains therapeutic concentration of Vajradanti, Lavang & Amla for anti-bacterial & anti-plaque activity, thereby inhibits salivary bacteria.

What is Usage & Presentation of GUM TONE?

DOSAGE: ½ teaspoonful of Gum Tone powder or Gum Tone gel, to be rubbed on the gums & teeth with fingers or a small brush & left for 2 – 3 minutes. Regular use in the morning & evening should be advised to the patient.
