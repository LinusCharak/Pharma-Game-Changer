What is Female Infertility?

Female Infertility - Infertility means not being able to get pregnant after at least one year of trying (or 6 months if the woman is over age 35). If a woman keeps having miscarriages, it is also called infertility. Female infertility can result from age, physical problems, hormone problems, and lifestyle or environmental factors.

 

What is Subfertility?

Subfertility - Subfertility generally describes any form of reduced fertility with prolonged time

What is the evidence to support the claims?

NUTRITIONAL SUPPLEMENTS like Vitamins, Antioxidants, Micronutrients, Hormones and Amino acids have shown to positively impact Fertility in several clinical studies

  • INTERNATIONAL FEDERATION of GYNECOLOGY and OBSTETRICS recommends that preconception micronutrient deficiencies are recognized and rectified through interventions including supplementation as appropriate.
  • AMERICAN JOURNAL of OBSTETRICS & GYNEACOLOGY says that Healthcare professionals should address optimal weight gain, healthy diet and the use of dietary supplements as a part of preconception care.
  • REPRODUCTIVE BIOMEDICINE ONLINE says that women who take adjuvant multiple micronutrient (MMN) supplementation during ovulation induction have a higher chance of pregnancy compared with women on folic acid
  • HUMAN REPRODUCTION says the concentration of Folate, Zinc, ROS and Thiols may have substantial effects on fertility and reproduction.
