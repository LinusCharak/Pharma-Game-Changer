2) WHAT IS STRESS?

Stress is the body’s natural response to physical, emotional, or mental pressure.

According to South Eastern European Journal of Public Health (SEEJPH), 2024:

  • 84% of people in India reported moderate to severe stress levels

If stress remains unmanaged, it can lead to:

  • Increased blood pressure
  • Cardiovascular disorders
  • Anxiety
  • Disturbed sleep
  • Mood fluctuations
  • Reduced cognitive function

3) WHAT IS CORTISOL?

Cortisol is the body's primary “stress hormone.”

It is:

  • Produced by the adrenal glands
  • Regulated by the HPA Axis (Hypothalamic–Pituitary–Adrenal Axis)

Under chronic stress:

  • CRH (Corticotropin Releasing Hormone) increases
  • ACTH (Adrenocorticotropic Hormone) increases
  • Cortisol levels rise

Elevated cortisol over time can damage physical and mental health.

 

4) WHAT IS HPA AXIS?

The HPA Axis (Hypothalamic–Pituitary–Adrenal Axis) is a neuroendocrine system that regulates the stress response.

If HPA axis is balanced:

  • Cortisol levels remain normal
  • Stress response is controlled

If HPA axis is disturbed:

  • Chronic cortisol elevation
  • Anxiety
  • Sleep disturbances
  • Hypertension
  • Reduced mental clarity

MENTACALM works by modulating the HPA axis.
