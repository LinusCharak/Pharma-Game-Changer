WHAT IS INFERTILITY?

  1. INFERTILITY means inability to produce children.
  2. Infertility can occur in men & women.
  3. In scientific terms, a couple is said to be infertile, when there is no conception (formation of the foetus inside the mother’s womb) after one year of regular intercourse without the use of contraceptives.
  4. Research has shown that in 40% of infertile couples, the problem lies in the male partner.
  5. Majority of these infertile males have some abnormality in their semen.
  6. SEMEN is the fluid that is ejaculated from the male reproductive organ during intercourse. It contains lakhs of sperms, which float inside the fluid of the semen. Defects in the structure & functions of the sperms are the most common cause of infertility.

HOW DO SPERM LEAD TO CONCEPTION?

 

During intercourse, semen is injected into the vagina. The sperms in the semen enter into the uterus through the vagina & swim upwards towards the fallopian tube. In the fallopian tube, any one of these sperms breaks the outer layer of the ovum (female egg) & then unites with it. This process is called FERTILIZATION OR CONCEPTION. For fertilization to occur, it is important that:

 

The structure of the sperms should be normal (as seen in the diagram above). The semen should have enough fluid in it (because the fluid gives nourishment to the sperms & also allows them to float freely). There should be enough number of sperms in the semen (more than 20 million sperms per milliliter of semen). A high number of sperms is required because most of the sperms die before they reach the ovum. However, even if one sperm is able to penetrate the ovum, fertilization will occur.

The sperms should be motile (move continuously). They will be able to swim towards the ovum only if they keep on moving continuously. When healthy sperms are seen under the microscope, it is observed that the tail (as seen in the diagram above) keeps on wagging continuously because of which the sperm swims forward.

The sperms must have enough energy supply to move & survive for several hours before uniting with the ovum.

 

WHAT ARE THE DEFECTS IN SPERMS THAT CAN LEAD TO INFERTILITY?

Infertility occurs if there is any defect in the five points mentioned above. Thus, infertility will occur if:

  • TERATOSPERMIA is a condition, wherein the structure of the sperms is not normal (“teratos” denotes a deformed structure in Greek language).
  • There is not enough fluid in the semen.
  • OLIGOSPERMIA means there are less number of sperms in the semen. A sperm count less than 20 million per milliliter is considered as oligospermia & it will lead to infertility.
  • AZOOSPERMIA is a condition, where there are no sperms at all.
  • ASTHENOSPERMIA - The sperms are not motile, More than 40% of the sperms should move continuously. If this percentage is less, fertilization will not occur.
  • The sperms do not have enough energy supply.

 

WHAT ARE THE CAUSES OF SUCH DEFECTS IN THE SPERMS?

The defects mentioned in the five points given above can be caused by the following:

  • Increased oxidative stress (oxidative stress us a condition in which the production of free radicals increase in the body. Free radicals are atoms or molecules with an extra electron. Free radicals are generated in the body during metabolism. If their number increases, they cause damage to the cells of the body).
  • Infections in the male reproductive organs
  • Diseases such as varicocele (in which the veins in the scrotum become swollen because of blockage)
  • Certain defects in the process of formation of sperms inside the testes.
  • Amongst all these causes, free radicals are an important cause of sperm defects as they can directly damage the sperms, resulting in deformities, reduced motility, disturbed energy supply & interference with the process of uniting with the ovum.

 

HOW CAN DEFECTS IN THE SPERMS BE PREVENTED?

Reduction in the level of free radicals in the semen is very important for preventing damage & defects in the sperms. If the damage is caused by underlying diseases such as varicocele or infection, then they should also be taken care of. Since antioxidants neutralize free radicals from the body, they are very useful in treating male infertility.

 

WHAT IS HPG AXIS?

The hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis is a neuroendocrine system that regulates reproductive processes in the body

If HPG axis is in balance then sperm count will be optimal. If HPG axis gets disturbed then patient will suffer with Oligospermia that is low sperm count.
