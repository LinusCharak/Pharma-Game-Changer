EXTRAMMUNE

(The ideal complement to antibiotic therapy)

 

What is the Product- one line Description?

EXTRAMMUNE is a immunity enhancing complement to anti-microbial therapy.

 

What are the Indications and its meaning?

EXTRAMMUNE is used in the following treatment modes:

PROPHYLACTIC MODE: As an immunity builder in:

Diabetics or patients on long-term oral steroid therapy.

Seasonal outbreak of infective disease caused by: Flu, Typhoid & Malaria.

Recurrent infections such as LRTI, URTI, SSTI, PUO and Viral Infections.

THERAPEUTIC MODE: To complement antibiotic therapy particularly in RTIs & SSTIs

 

LRTI – Lower Respiratory Tract Infection

URTI – Upper Respiratory Tract Infection

SSTI – Skin & Soft Tissue Infection

PUO – Pyrexia(Fever) of Unknown Origin

What is PROPHYLACTIC and THERAPEUTIC use?

A medicine or course of action used to prevent disease or infection is known as PROPHYLACTIC use. 

A medicine or course of action used to treat disease or infection which person already suffering is known as THERAPEUTIC use.
