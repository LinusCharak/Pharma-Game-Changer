POSEX FORTE

(Arrests Bleeding Effectively and Safely)

 

WHAT IS POSEX FORTE?

Posex is a natural haemostatic medicine.

 

WHAT IS IT FOR?

In gynecology

  • To decrease the severity and duration of excessive menstrual bleeding
  • To manage the bleeding after childbirth
  • To treat bleeding after insertion of intra-uterine contraceptive devices (IUCD) (for example, copper T) and after medical termination of pregnancy (MTP)
  • To treat menstrual bleeding occurring before menopause
  • To treat bleeding from piles during pregnancy

 

In General Medicine/Surgery

  • Before surgery, to prevent chances of excessive bleeding
  • To treat epistaxis (bleeding from the nose)
  • To treat haemoptysis (blood in the sputum)
  • To treat haematemesis (vomit of blood)
  • To treat bleeding associated with haemorrhoids (piles)

 

HOW DOES POSEX WORK?

  • Exhibits Astringent activity
  • Corrects Capillary fragility
  • Arrests Hemorrhages due to Khadir, Amalaki & Lodhra
  • Exerts contraction of Uterine muscles
  • Normalizes FSH (follicle stimulating hormone) & LH (leutenizing hormone) levels

WHAT ARE THE UNIQUE PRESCRIBING?

  • Posex Forte is a comprehensive formulation of time tested ingredients of superior efficacy to arrest bleeding immediately and sustain the haemostyptic effect
  • Contains ingredients such as Acacia catechu, Mimosa pudica, Symplocos racemosa and Bombax malabaricum which are natural astringents and haemostatics.
  • These ingredients activate the proteins that are required for the process of clot formation.
  • They also improve the strength of the walls of the small arteries and veins (i.e. they reduce the capillary fragility).
  • The other ingredients of Posex Forteenhance the coagulatory properties of the main haemostatic agents.

 

WHAT IS THE DOSAGE & PRESENTATION OF POSEX FORTE?

DOSAGE

2 capsules thrice a day or 2 capsules every two hourly, up to a maximum of 12 capsules in 24 hours, till bleeding stops.

Pre-operative: 2 capsules  thrice  a  day  commencing  three  days  prior  to  operation/ procedure, and continuing for further two days after the operation/procedure is over.

 

PRESENTATION

Blister pack of 20 capsules.
