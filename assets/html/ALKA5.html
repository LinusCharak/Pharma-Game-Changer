Mechanism of action

Dysuria is attributed to acidic urine, due to bacterial infection

This may precipitate pain and burning during micturation in UTI

Urinary Alkalizer is a medication to reduce acidity of urine

It helps relieve burning micturation and soothes inflamed urinary mucosa.

 

WHAT ARE THE UNIQUE PRESCRIBING REASONS (UPRs) FOR ALKA 5?

  • Soothes burning micturition
  • Prevents dysuria, reduces pain & inflammation
  • Reduces lower abdominal pain
  • Potentiates the action of antibacterials
  • Prevents recolonization & recurrent UTIs
  • No need to dilute with water
  • Delicious taste ensures better patient compliance
  • Unlike sodium citrate or potassium citrate, can be used safely for cardiac patients.
