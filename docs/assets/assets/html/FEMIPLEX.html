What is Vaginitis?

Vaginitis is an inflammation of the vaginal mucosa.

When the vulva is also inflamed, it is called Vulvo-Vaginitis. (The vulva is the fold of skin covering the opening to the vagina.)

Vaginitis can occur in female of any age.

 

What are the various categories of Vaginitis?

INFECTIOUS VAGINITIS accounts for 90% of all cases in reproductive age women & is usually caused by Candida albicans (a fungus), Trichomonas vaginalis (a protozoan) or by Gardnerella (a bacterium).

 

HORMONAL VAGINITIS is usually found in postmenopausal, or postpartum women. Sometimes it can occur in young girls before puberty.

 

IRRITANT VAGINITIS can be caused by allergies to condoms, spermicides, soaps, perfumes, douches & semen. It can also be caused by hot tubs, abrasion, tissue, tampons or topical medications.

 

What are the symptoms of Vaginitis?

  • Irritation & itching of the genital area
  • Inflammation of the labia majora, labia minora, or perineal area
  • Vaginal discharge
  • Foul vaginal odor
  • Discomfort or burning when urinating
  • Bloody discharge
