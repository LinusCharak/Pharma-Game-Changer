What is Endometriosis?

Endometriosis is defined as the presence of endometrial tissue in an abnormal location (i.e. outside the uterus- ovary, fallopian tubes, back or front of uterus, etc.)

 

What does ENDOTONE do?

ENDOTONE is an efficacious herbal formulation for the management of endometriosis. ENDOTONE effectively delays and stops the proliferation of endometrial cells. It reduces endometriosis associated pain such as dysmenorrhea, dyspareunia etc. Further it also restores the fertility, reduces the emotional distress and improves quality of life.

 

How does it work? Mechanism of Action

  • Curcuma longa inhibits the proliferation of endometrial cells in endometriosis by reducing the estradiol production. It also arrests endometriosis by down-regulating the matrix metalloproteinase-9 (MMP-9) and matrix metalloproteinase-2 (MMP-2) activity. This action further decreases the expression of TNF-α resulting into anti-inflammatory effect. Curcum longa has been found to decrease the vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) and micro vessel density (MVD) in the ectopic endometrium.
  • Cyperus rotundus effectively reduces dysmenorrhea, pain and inflammation. Studies have shown that Cyperus rotundus reduces the incidence rate of surgery in endometriosis patients.
  • Zingiber officinale reduces menstrual pain and cramps by inhibiting the prostaglandin biosynthesis. Cinnamomum cassia also gives relief from menstrual cramps, dysmenorrhea and menorrhagia.
  • Symplocos racemosa effectively treats endometriosis, stops uterine bleeding, reduces pain, inflammation and improves fertility.
  • Withania somnifera and Glycyrrhiza glabra help to induce sleep, uplift mood, and reduce anxiety.
