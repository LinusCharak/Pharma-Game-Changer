WHAT IS GALAKOL?

GALAKOL is an herbal galactogogue formulation that initiates, augments and maintains lactation.

 

WHAT IS THE INDICATION FOR GALAKOL?

GALAKOL is indicated in inadequate lactation due to subnormal physiological status or postpartum stress.

 

WHAT DOES GALAKOL DO?

  • GALAKOL is an herbal formulation that initiates, augments and maintains lactation for prolonged duration.
  • Asparagus racemosus (Shatavari) has estrogenic effect and lactogogue property.
  • Extract of Shatavari has been shown to increase both the weight of mammary lobulo-aveolar tissue and the milk yield. This effect was attributed to the action of released corticosteroids or an increase in prolactin.
  • Ipomoea digitata (Vidarikand) and other ingredients act as nutritional supplements.
  • GALAKOL improves general health status and well-being.
