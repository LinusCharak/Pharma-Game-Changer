What is Osteoarthritis?

Osteoarthritis is not a single disease but rather the end result of a variety of disorders leading to the structural or functional failure of one or more of joints. Osteoarthritis involves the entire joint including the nearby muscles, underlying bone, ligaments, joint lining (synovium), and the joint cover (capsule).

 

What are the signs and symptoms of Osteoarthritis?

Osteoarthritis is a chronic condition that causes joint pain, stiffness, and difficulty moving the joints. Other symptoms include:

  • Swelling in and around the joint
  • Feeling that the joint is loose or unstable
  • A grating or crackling sound or sensation in the joints
  • Joints appearing slightly larger or more "knobbly" than usual
  • Weakness and loss of muscle bulk
  • Sleep disturbances, such as insomnia
