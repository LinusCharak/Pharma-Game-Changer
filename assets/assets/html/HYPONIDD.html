What is Diabetes Mellitus (DM)?

  • DIABETES MELLITUS (DM) is a common endocrine disease, characterized by an increased amount of glucose (sugar) in the blood.
  • It is caused when the Beta (β) cells of the pancreas cannot produce enough INSULIN to metabolize the glucose in the blood.
  • It is also called as a metabolic disease, as it is characterized by metabolic abnormalities & long-term complications involving the eyes, kidneys, nerves & blood vessels.

 

What is Impaired Glucose Tolerance (IGT)?

  • IMPAIRED GLUCOSE TOLERANCE (IGT) is a transition phase between normal glucose tolerance & Diabetes.
  • In this condition, the levels of blood glucose are between normal & diabetic.
  • People with IGT do not have diabetes.
  • IGT may precede Type 2 DM by many years.
  • IGT is also a risk factor for mortality.
  • Although some drugs can delay the onset of diabetes, lifestyle modifications such as Weight loss & exercise may help, in bringing the glucose levels to normal.

 

What is PCOS?

POLYCYSTIC OVARY SYNDROME (PCOS) or PCOD is a disease of the female reproductive system. The earliest sign of PCOS is disturbance in menses (menstruation). A patient will be defined as suffering from PCOS if she has the following 3 problems:

  • OVULATORY DISORDER where there is a problem in the formation of the female egg, i.e. ovum in the ovary.
  • INCREASE IN THE LEVEL OF ANDROGENS (male hormones) in the blood. It is important to note that under normal conditions also, there are male hormones in the female body & vice-versa. These hormones of the opposite sex are kept under control by certain enzymes in the body. If the proportion of these hormones of the opposite sex increases in the body, it will produce physical characteristics of the opposite sex. E.g. In PCOS, if the proportion of TESTOSTERONE (male hormone) increases in the female body, it produces male characteristics such as excessive hair on the body.
  • PRESENCE OF SEVERAL CYSTS IN THE OVARY. These can be seen by performing a sonography or laparoscopy.
