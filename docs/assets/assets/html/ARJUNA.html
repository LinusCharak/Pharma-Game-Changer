What are the indications in which arjuna+ can be promoted?

Arjuna (Terminalia arjuna) helps manage cholesterol levels, and one of its key actions is believed to involve the inhibition of the enzyme HMG-CoA reductase — the same enzyme targeted by statin drugs.

What is HMG-CoA Reductase?

  • It's a key enzyme your liver uses to produce cholesterol.
  • Blocking or reducing this enzyme activity helps lower LDL (bad cholesterol) and total cholesterol.

How Arjuna Acts on HMG-CoA Reductase:

  • Arjuna bark extract contains polyphenols, flavonoids, and saponins.
  • These compounds have shown HMG-CoA reductase inhibitory activity in lab studies.
  • This leads to:
    • ↓ Decreased cholesterol synthesis
    • ↓ LDL cholesterol
    • ↑ HDL (good cholesterol)
    • ↓ Triglycerides

Overall Benefit:

Arjuna naturally supports lipid balance and protects against heart diseases, without the common side effects associated with synthetic statins.
