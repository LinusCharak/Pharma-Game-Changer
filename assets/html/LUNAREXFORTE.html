WHAT IS LUNAREX FORTE?

Lunarex is a natural formulation, which initiates and regulates menstruation.

 

WHAT ARE THE INDICATIONS FOR LUNAREX FORTE?

Oligomenorrhoea or hypomenorrhoea

Secondary amenorrhoea

Primary amenorrhoea without the evidence of any congenital or anatomical abnormality

 

WHAT IS THE MODE OF ACTION OF LUNAREX FORTE?

Initiates & Regulates menstrual flow

Improves Blood supply to pelvic organs

Induces menstrual flow

Stimulates Ovulation

Improves Ovarian functions

WHAT ARE THE UNIQUE PRESCRIBING REASONS OF LUNAREX FORTE?

  • Lunarex is non-invasive approach to menstrual modulation
  • Lunarex induces and regulate menstruation naturally
  • Lunarex is safe option without the risks associated with Hormonal treatment
  • No risk of epiphyseal ossification which can retard the growth
  • It contains highest concentration of Aloe vera 300mg
  • Also contains Abroma augusta and Daucus carota, the two important ingredients for stimulating ovarian function.

 

WHAT IS THE DOSAGE & PRESENTATION OF LUNAREX FORTE?

Dosage

Oligomenorrhoea:- 1 capsule twice daily to be started a week before expected date of menstruation.

The medication should be discontinued during menstruation and repeated in each cycle till regular rhythm is restored.

Amenorrhoea: 1 capsule twice daily till menstruation sets in.

CAUTION

Rule out pregnancy before prescribing. Discontinue during menstruation

PRESENTATION:

Blister pack of 20 capsules.
