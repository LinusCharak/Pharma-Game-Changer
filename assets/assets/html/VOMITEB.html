What are the evidences to support the claims?

Clinical trial 1:

Management of vomiting of pregnancy with Vomiteb - Gujar M. Current Medical Practice.

200 cases of vomiting during pregnancy were studied. Of these, 150 were treated with Vomiteb caspsule  in the dose of 2 tablets four times a day and the rest 50 were put on a placebo for a period of four weeks.  With Vomiteb capsule, a total relief was obtained in 78 % cases as against 20 % treated with placebo. No side effects with Vomiteb were noted.

Thus, Vomiteb has a useful place in the management of vomiting during pregnancy.

 

Clinical trial 2:

Vomiting of early pregnancy- A clinical and therapeutic evaluation - Shah LM. Current Medical Practice.

12(10): pp. 357-59  350 cases of vomiting during early pregnancy were studied. Patients were treated with Vomiteb capsule in the dose of one tablet three times a day for a period ranging from two to four weeks. With Vomiteb, a total relief was obtained in 73 % cases. No side effects were observed with Vomiteb.

 

Clinical trial 3:

Vomiteb in pediatric practice - Sharma A, Sharma V, Saxena S. The Medicine and Surgery.

100 children complaining of vomiting were studied. Of these, 91 were administered with Vomiteb syrup and the rest were put on placebo.  72.68 % patients of regurgitation, 85 % with acute gastroenteritis and 57.13 % with gastritis responded to Vomiteb therapy compared 9.62 % (regurgitation), 0 % (acute gastroenteritis) and 21.43 % (acute gastritis) with placebo.

 

Clinical trial 4:

Role of Vomiteb in treatment of vomiting due to chemotherapy of cancer.- Sanchette S and Sanchette A. The Medicine and Surgery,

The study was carried out in 20 cancer patients of different age groups. These patients were receiving radiotherapy and so complained about vomiting as a side effect. All the patients were given Vomiteb Capsule one and a half hour before the dose of radiation and 1 tablet six hourly for 2 days thereafter. 75 % patients treated with Vomiteb showed a complete relief from vomiting. In 20 % patients the frequency of vomiting reduced. No side effects of therapy were noted.

 

Clinical trial 5:

Vomiting in pediatric practice- clinical evaluation of therapeutic efficacy  of Vomiteb syrup. Shejwal MS. Current Medical Practice.

100 children including infants where vomiting was a symptom was studied. Of these 50 patients were put on Vomiteb syrup and the rest were administered metoclopramide syrup.  88 % of the patients benefited with Vomiteb syrup as compared to 64 % with metoclopramide syrup within one to three days of therapy. No side effects were noted with Vomiteb, whereas patients with metoclopramide exhibited side effects like drowsiness and extrapyramidal reaction.

 

Clinical trial 6:

Comparative evaluation of Vomiteb syrup versus Domperidone in the management of vomiting in pediatric practice. - Shankar A. Doctors News.

400 children with nausea and vomiting were studied. Of these 200 were put on Vomiteb syrup and the rest 200 received Domperidone syrup.   90 % children on Vomiteb syrup had cessation of nausea and vomiting without any relapse whereas in 91 % children treated with Domperidone, nausea persisted and 42% had relapse.
